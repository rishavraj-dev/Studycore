StudyCore Help Center

Last Updated: June 26, 2025

Welcome to the StudyCore Help Center! Here, you'll find solutions to common issues and answers to frequently asked questions to ensure a smooth learning experience on our platform. If you can't find what you're looking for, our support team is ready to assist you.

Common Troubleshooting Steps

1. Account & Login Issues

Q: I can't log in to my account. What should I do?
A:

  1. Double-check your email address and password for any typos.
  2. Ensure Caps Lock is not on.
  3. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it.
  4. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then try logging in again.
  5. Try logging in from a different browser or device.

Q: My old account from `studycore.infinityfreeapp.com` isn't working. Why?
A: The new StudyCore (studycore.kesug.com) is an entirely new platform. Accounts from `studycore.infinityfreeapp.com` do not automatically transfer. You need to create a new account on this platform. We encourage you to register and explore the enhanced features!

2. Content Access Problems

Q: I can't access certain study materials or test series. What's wrong?
A:

  1. Ensure you are logged into your StudyCore account.
  2. Check your subscription status. Some premium content and advanced test series require an active paid subscription. You can review your plan details in your profile.
  3. Verify your internet connection.
  4. Refresh the page or clear your browser's cache.

Q: The videos or interactive elements are not loading.
A:

  1. Check your internet connection speed and stability.
  2. Ensure your browser is up-to-date.
  3. Disable any ad-blockers or browser extensions that might interfere with content loading.
  4. Try accessing the content in an incognito/private browser window.

3. Technical Issues & Performance

Q: The website is running slowly or freezing.
A:

  1. Close any other unnecessary tabs or applications running on your device.
  2. Clear your browser's cache and cookies.
  3. Check your internet connection. A slow or unstable connection can affect performance.
  4. Restart your browser or device.

Q: I encountered an error message. What does it mean?
A: If you see an error message, try taking a screenshot or noting down the exact message. Then, try the action again. If the error persists, please provide the error message and details of what you were doing when it occurred when contacting support.

4. Subscription & Billing

Q: How do I manage my subscription or update billing information?
A: You can manage your subscription details, update payment methods, and review your billing history by logging into your account and navigating to the "Subscription" or "Billing" section in your user dashboard.

Q: I was charged incorrectly / My payment failed.
A: Please check your bank statement or payment method details first. If you believe there's an error, contact our support team immediately with your transaction details and a description of the issue. We'll investigate promptly.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find a solution to your problem here, our dedicated support team is ready to assist you further. Please reach out to us, providing as much detail as possible about your issue.
