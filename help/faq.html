Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Last Updated: June 26, 2025

Welcome to StudyCore! This FAQ section aims to answer your common questions about the new StudyCore platform, including the transition from our previous service, login requirements, and our new free and paid features.

General Questions

Q: What is the new StudyCore platform?
A: StudyCore is your upgraded online learning platform, designed to help students excel in Class 10, 11, 12, JEE, and NEET. This new platform offers enhanced features, a smoother user interface, and exclusive content, providing a more robust and personalized learning experience.

Q: Who is StudyCore for?
A: StudyCore is primarily for students preparing for:

  • Class 10 Board Exams
  • Class 11 & 12 Board Exams
  • Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main & Advanced)
  • National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET)

Q: Is StudyCore free to use?
A: StudyCore operates on a freemium model. We offer a selection of valuable free features and content to get you started. For complete access to our advanced study materials, comprehensive test series, and premium tools, you can opt for one of our paid subscription plans.

Account & Access

Q: How do I create an account on the new StudyCore?
A: Simply click on the "Register" button in the navigation bar on our homepage. You'll be guided through a quick signup process where you can create your new StudyCore account using your email address and a password.

Q: Do I need to log in to access content?
A: Yes, the new StudyCore platform is login-based. While some introductory content and features might be visible without logging in, a free or paid account is required to access most study materials, test series, and personalized features.

Q: I had an account on `studycore.infinityfreeapp.com`. Can I use it here?
A: Welcome back! The new StudyCore platform is a completely re-engineered and upgraded service. Your old account from `studycore.infinityfreeapp.com` **will not directly transfer** to this new platform. You will need to create a **new account** here. We have a special offer for our legacy users; please check your registered email or contact support for details.

Q: What if I forget my password?
A: Don't worry! On the login page, click on "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions. You'll be able to reset your password via your registered email address.

Features & Content

Q: What kind of study materials are available?
A: We offer a wide range of study materials including detailed notes, chapter-wise questions, solved examples, concept videos, and more, covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics for your respective exams.

Q: What are the StudyCore Test Series?
A: Our advanced test series provides realistic exam simulations, allowing you to practice under timed conditions. Each test comes with detailed performance analysis, answer explanations, and insights into your strengths and weaknesses to help you improve.

Q: What do I get with a premium StudyCore subscription?
A: Premium subscriptions unlock:

  • Full access to our entire library of premium study materials.
  • Unlimited attempts at all advanced test series for JEE, NEET, and Board exams.
  • Detailed analytics and personalized progress tracking.
  • Priority customer support.
  • And more exclusive features designed for comprehensive preparation!

Q: What features are available for free users?
A: Free users can:

  • Create an account and explore the platform.
  • Access introductory study materials for each subject/class.
  • Attempt a limited number of sample tests.
  • Get a glimpse of our analytics features.
  • Access basic customer support.

Technical & Support

Q: What are the system requirements for using StudyCore?
A: StudyCore is a web-based platform, accessible via any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) on desktop, laptop, or mobile devices. An active internet connection is required.

Q: How can I contact StudyCore support?
A: You can reach our support team by visiting the "Contact Us" section on our website (link in the footer), or by sending an email to [support@studycore.com - Replace with your actual email]. We aim to respond within 24-48 hours.
